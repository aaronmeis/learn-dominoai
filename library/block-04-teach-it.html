Block 4: Teach it

Block 4 — Teach it. Five minutes to a laptop-notebook person.

Time: 30 minutes
Goal: Speak the sketch
Pack: Domino Data Lab (dominoai)

Focus

Five minutes to someone who has trained a notebook model on a laptop. Must include one sentence on why a system of record is the product, not the IDE. Must include one sentence on a job Domino should refuse (or lose to a competitor). Watch [S12] or [S13] only if you still cannot say it.

Sources

  • Your block 3 sketch, [S4], [S13], CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES Debate 1 and Debate 5

Actions

  1. Outline: what it is (30s), planes (60s), lifecycle (90s), refusal (60s), IL5 caveat (30s).
  2. Record or speak it once. Note the sentence that stalled.
  3. That stall becomes a quiz row.

Retrieval check
