3. Five killer questions (20-second answers)

1. Why now?

  • Spoken Answer: We must transition from treating data science as isolated laptop research projects to establishing systemic enterprise MLOps lineage. Unanchored notebook development prevents model auditability, creates compliance risks, and blocks seamless transition from experimentation to production [S13].
  • Elevate Line: Systemic MLOps governance must replace fragmented research habits to make model outcomes reproducible, auditable, and enterprise-ready.

2. What if we wait? (Dated cost of no)

  • Spoken Answer: Delaying platform placement accumulates severe unpinned environment drift, ungoverned production deployments, and unchecked GPU infrastructure burn caused by interactive IDE workspaces left running idle on high-tier Kubernetes node pools [S5], [S6].
  • Elevate Line: Inaction compounds regulatory audit exposure and unbudgeted infrastructure spend across disparate development teams.

3. Who else has done this? (Benchmark or analogue)

  • Spoken Answer: Production deployment architectures presented at RevX demonstrate unified MLOps hierarchies and MLflow-backed model card governance [S12], [S13]. Academic literature evaluates underlying open-source components like MLflow ([S14]) rather than primary commercial Domino benchmarks.
  • Elevate Line: Proven architectural patterns validate the model card approach, though empirical productivity claims must be baselined internally.

4. What are we stopping? (Capacity / tradeoff)

  • Spoken Answer: We are halting redundant internal control plane engineering and stopping unmonitored interactive workspace sessions that consume expensive GPU hardware tiers without generating tracked artifacts, reallocating capacity toward standardized Model Sentry governance [S2], [S6], [S7].
  • Elevate Line: Trade custom control plane engineering and idle compute burn for standardized, automated system-of-record governance.

5. What if this slips? (Risk and recovery)

  • Spoken Answer: If platform integration slips, development continues safely in local data planes or via separate Kubernetes node pools within the same cluster, maintaining environment isolation without blocking data science research or risking cross-stage contamination [S7].
  • Elevate Line: Multi-plane and node-pool isolation ensures ongoing research continuity while staging and production governance controls are finalized.
