Mission — Domino Data Lab (Enterprise AI Platform)

Mission for one-day mastery of Domino Data Lab (Enterprise AI Platform).

Business Explanation

Business Explanation

This day is for placing Domino, not clicking through a tenant. Aaron should leave able to explain what a Project contains, where compute runs, what the system of record is supposed to record, and which competitor wins which job.

Why

Working fluency in Domino Data Lab (domino.ai) so a design-review room can hear a placement, not a feature list.

Success looks like

  • Explain from zero: model, training vs inference, experiment, registry, serving, MLOps, governance, Kubernetes at outline
  • Sketch one governed lifecycle: Project → Workspace / Job → Experiment → Registry → Endpoint or App, naming where compute runs and what is recorded
  • Name one job Domino should refuse, and one named failure mode
  • Teach that sketch in five minutes

Out of scope

  • Production deploy, live tenant or trial, packet capture
  • Treating IL5 / HIPAA / SOC 2 / 21 CFR Part 11 as an authorization
  • Building custom Docker Environments as a lab
  • Multi-week academy depth

Constraints

  • Time box: ~350 focused minutes inside eight hours
  • Ground claims in RESOURCES / source-ledger
  • Vendor percentages and compliance logos stay qualified until a primary page or a measurement supports them
  • Video renders are a sub-run for another agent (AGENT-HANDOFF - Video renders)
