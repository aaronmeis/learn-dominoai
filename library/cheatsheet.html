Cheatsheet — Domino Data Lab

One-page decision card for the Domino Data Lab study day.

Choose When
Domino You need a governed lifecycle (Project → experiment → registry → endpoint or App) across IDEs, and compute may sit in more than one cluster. [S1] [S2] [S4]
Databricks The lakehouse and Unity Catalog already own the data and the registry. Nexus is a second control plane. Debate 5.
SageMaker The account is AWS-native and endpoints already exist. Export from Domino is a handoff, not a second home. [S7]
OpenShift AI The estate is already OpenShift and the team will operate that stack.
Kubeflow The team will own CRDs and does not want a commercial control plane.
Dataiku The users are analysts on a visual workbench, not IDE-first scientists.
Trap Tell Fix
Hosted Jupyter is the product People never open a model card The reconstructable unit is the card. [S4]
Reproducibility theater Files tracked, Environment revision missing Pin Environment + data version. [S5] [S4]

Any-tool = no lock-in "We can always export" Export the artifact. The metadata stores stay. [S2] [S5]
IL5 logo = ATO Homepage sentence Demand a PA / ATO page. [S10]
Briefing percentages 50% / 6× / 40% / 75% Teach the mechanism. Measure your queue. [S10]
Remote plane = local plane Publish an App on a remote plane Read the installed Nexus page. [S3]
App Hub vs Apps Pitch deck name Contract is Publish Apps, port 8888. [S8]
