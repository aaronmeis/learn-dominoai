The claim

Domino is an enterprise AI platform whose product is the recorded context (Project, Environment, experiment, model card), not the IDE window. Compute runs in a data plane. Metadata lives in a control plane. Nexus adds planes. An IL5 logo is not an ATO. Briefing percentages are not an SLA.
