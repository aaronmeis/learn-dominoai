After a sitting

  1. Log the hole on Progress Log (three things you could not recall, one new source, or one unrun evidence ID).
  2. Write the vault: GLOSSARY, quiz-bank, source-ledger, watch-and-reading, CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES (warrants and designed tests when the hole is "I could not defend why").
  3. Evidence sitting (follow-on). If the hole is an unrun test, follow AGENT-HANDOFF - Evidence sittings. Desk tests first. Lab tests need a tenant GO. File the result on the debate row and the tracker.
  4. Ask an agent /learn-console to curate js/data.js (DATA.debates status pills), rebuild library/claims-debates.html, and push main.
  5. Hard-reload the phone on #/debates. Do not poll Pages.

Do not generate extra NotebookLM reports unless the sitting names a hole the current reports do not cover. W8 execute waits on nlm login unless Aaron asks.

Point another agent at: AGENT-HANDOFF - Evidence sittings (desk tests) or AGENT-HANDOFF - Video renders (HyperFrames, Remotion, TDD cut).
