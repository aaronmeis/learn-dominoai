Study console for the Domino Data Lab one-day pack: governed lifecycle, control plane versus data plane, model cards, Apps, Nexus, and when another platform is better.

**Live:** https://aaronmeis.github.io/learn-dominoai/

**Pack:** `Learning/one-day-mastery/dominoai/` in the Obsidian vault. The source ledger on the site matches that note. NotebookLM reports, when they arrive, are secondary. IL5 is not an ATO. Homepage percentages are not an SLA.

## What's here

- Today: a daily challenge from the 12-slide spine, failure modes, and placement drills.
- Learn: eleven study blocks, the deck (slides after W8), shorts (after the video agent), and the library of vault notes.
- Practice: glossary flashcards, the quiz, and study prompts.
- Reference: cheatsheet, glossary, debates with run/unrun pills, and the 15-row source ledger.

Storage prefix is `lda:` because this origin is shared with other learn-* sites. No Python, no operator scripts, and no credentials live in this repo.
